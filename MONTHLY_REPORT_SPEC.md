# Monthly Treasury Allocation Report Spec

## Schedule

- Run monthly on the 5th at 9:00 AM local time.
- Send the completed report to `ghkjs0810@gmail.com`.

## Contribution Assumption

Unless the user provides a different amount, assume the investable Treasury contribution is 30% of monthly salary.

The report must show:

- The ratio within the Treasury contribution, such as `45% short / 35% medium / 20% long`.
- The equivalent percentage of monthly salary, such as `13.5% / 10.5% / 6.0%`.
- A concise action summary.

## Treasury Buckets

- Short-term U.S. government Treasuries.
- Medium-term U.S. government Treasuries.
- Long-term U.S. government Treasuries.

## Required Macro Context

Use the latest available global macroeconomic landscape, including:

- U.S. Treasury yield curve conditions.
- Federal Reserve policy expectations.
- Inflation trends.
- Growth and recession indicators.
- Labor market signals.
- Credit and liquidity stress.
- Major central bank direction.
- Currency, geopolitical, and global bond market risks.

## Source Requirements

Every factual rationale point must include source links or source notes directly in the rationale section.

Prioritize primary or official sources:

- Federal Reserve.
- U.S. Treasury or Federal Reserve H.15.
- Bureau of Labor Statistics.
- Bureau of Economic Analysis.
- IMF.
- ECB.
- Bank of Japan.
- OECD.
- Other primary market or macro data providers when relevant.

Include a final `Sources` section listing all links used.

## Compliance Language

The report must state that it is decision support, not personalized financial advice.
